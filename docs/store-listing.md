## Full description
Real ink, moving on your home screen.

Inkwell turns your wallpaper into a vessel of liquid. Touch it and ink falls, blooms and unfurls, then drifts and settles on its own. Every drop is simulated, so no two moments repeat.

HOW IT FEELS
• Tap for a single drop. Hold for a thin, steady thread. Drag to stir the liquid.
• Choose how the ink behaves: Swirl, Bloom, Ring, Burst, Thread or Mist.
• Drops drift in by themselves, so the wallpaper is alive even when you aren't touching it.
• A fresh drop falls each time you wake your phone.

11 HAND-PICKED THEMES
Porcelain, Sumi, Tide, Abyss, Crema, Incense, Aurora, Blush, Ember, Orchid and Moonstone. Pale liquids that ink darkens, dark liquids where ink glows, and creamy clouds in coffee-dark water. Each has its own named inks, such as Prussian, Vermilion and Sumi.

TUNE IT YOUR WAY
Weight, swirl, linger, drop size and flow, plus light, glow and caustics. Make the ink sink, float or rise.

GENTLE ON YOUR BATTERY
Rendering pauses whenever the wallpaper isn't on screen and slows down when it settles.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no accounts, no internet connection and no data collection.

Requires a phone with OpenGL ES 3.0 (most phones from the last several years).
